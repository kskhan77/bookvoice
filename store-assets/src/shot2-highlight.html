Chapter One · The Quiet Power of Daily Reading

Every reader begins somewhere, and it is rarely with a grand plan. A few pages before bed, a chapter with morning coffee — these small moments accumulate quietly.

What matters is not how quickly you finish a book, but how deeply its ideas settle into your thinking.

Listening is reading too. When a voice carries the words, your eyes are free to follow, and comprehension often grows rather than shrinks.
