# BookVoice - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 13, 2026_

BookVoice is a browser extension that reads web pages aloud using a
text-to-speech model that runs entirely on your own device.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** BookVoice does not collect, transmit, store remotely, sell, or
share any data. It has no servers, no analytics, no accounts, and no
telemetry.

## How your data is handled

- **Page text**: When you click "Read page", the text of the current page is
  extracted and converted to speech **locally on your device**. It is never
  sent anywhere and is discarded when reading stops.
- **Settings**: Your chosen voice and reading speed are saved in your
  browser's local extension storage on your device only.
- **Voice model**: On first use, the extension downloads the open-source
  Kokoro voice model files (data weights, not code) from huggingface.co.
  This is a plain file download; no information about you or the pages you
  read is included in the request. The model is cached locally afterwards
  and the extension works offline.

## Permissions

BookVoice requests access to page content solely to read the text of pages
you explicitly ask it to read aloud. It does not monitor your browsing.
